Hadir for desktop

Take Hadir to your own computer.

Free. Your classes, students and grades stay on your computer, in a folder you own. Nothing to host and nothing to pay for.

Installed in four steps

  1. 1

    Download

    Use the green button above. The installer is about 95 MB.

  2. 2

    Open it

    Windows may say "Windows protected your PC", because the app is new and not yet signed. Choose More info, then Run anyway.

  3. 3

    Create your account

    Enter your name and email, add your title and subjects, and pick a colour. Save the recovery code it shows you: it is how you get back in if you forget your password.

  4. 4

    Add a class

    Create a group, paste your student list or let students join with a QR code, and start your first roll call.

How your students connect

A phone only creates its signing key on a secure https address, so students need a link that opens on https. You set it up once.

  1. Make a free static address that points to your computer, for example an ngrok static domain.
  2. In Hadir, open Settings, Student address, and paste it.
  3. Open Student access, show the class QR code, and students join from their phones.
  4. From then on, roll call needs no internet in the room: students show their code, you scan it.

What you get

Offline roll call

Signed QR codes, checked on your device. A screenshot cannot fake one.

Your own portal

Your title, university, subjects and colour. Colleagues can create their own accounts on the same computer, and cannot see yours.

Grades and excuses

Grades out of 20 with CEFR levels, and excuses with proof you approve in one tap.

Automatic follow-up

Alerts after 2 and 3 absences, deadline reminders and a weekly digest. Formal notices wait for your approval.

Reports

The semester on one sheet. Print it or open the CSV in Excel.

Private by design

Data stays in your folder. Passwords are hashed, accounts lock after repeated failures, and there is a recovery code if you forget yours.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no hosting, no database to rent and no subscription. It runs on your computer.

Where is my data?

In your app-data folder, for example %APPDATA%\Hadir on Windows. Use Hadir, Back up now, in the app menu, to copy it somewhere safe.

I forgot my password.

Choose Forgot your password on the sign-in page and enter your email, your recovery code and a new password. No code? The owner of the computer can issue a one-time code from Settings.

Does roll call need internet?

No. Students join once, online. After that the codes are made on their phones and checked on your computer, in a room with no network at all.

Can colleagues use it too?

Yes. Each educator creates an account on the same computer, or installs their own copy. Educators never see each other's classes.
