Signed on this phone. The key never leaves it.

The internet is optional in the room.

Only the first and last steps use the network. The scan itself never does.

  1. Once, online

    Join and download

    Each student joins with the code you give them. Their phone makes a signing key that never leaves it. Your phone downloads the class list and the public keys.

  2. In the room, no network

    Show, scan, done

    The student shows their code. You scan it. Your phone checks the signature, the time and the class list in a blink, then marks them present or late.

  3. After class, online

    Upload and follow up

    Scans upload in one batch. The server checks them again, marks everyone missing as absent, and your rules send the alerts.

Why a screenshot can't fake it

Students can say their network failed. With Hadir it doesn't matter, because nothing in the room depends on it.

  • Signed on the student's phone

    The key is created on the phone and cannot be exported, so a code cannot be moved to another device.

  • New every 30 seconds

    Your phone rejects a code older than 45 seconds. A screenshot sent from outside the room is already stale.

  • You are in the room

    You scan the screen yourself, so you see who is holding the phone.

  • One student, one scan

    A second scan is ignored. A new phone has to be approved by you before it counts.

Rules you can read aloud

  • When a student reaches 2 absences, warn them and tell me.
